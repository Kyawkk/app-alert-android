An alarm for the notifications you can't miss.

Pick the apps that matter. When one of them notifies you, App Alert rings, even when your phone is on silent or you're away from the screen.

Features

Loud when it matters, quiet when it doesn't.

Choose your apps

Monitor only the apps you pick, each with its own on and off switch.

Per-app sounds

Use a system ringtone or your own audio file, with volume, duration, repeat and vibration.

Smart filters

Alarm only for keywords or notification types such as messages, calls or email.

Quiet hours

Stay silent during a daily window, even across midnight. Skipped alerts still show in history.

Alert history

See what rang and what was skipped. Only the outcome is kept, for 30 days.

Light, dark, adaptive

Follows your system theme or stays fixed, with layouts for phones and tablets.

Screens

Designed with Material 3.

Calm, readable and quick to set up.

Privacy

Your notifications never leave your phone.

App Alert has no server, no account and no analytics. Message text is read only to apply your filters and is never stored or logged. The whole project is open source under the MIT license, so you can check every line.
