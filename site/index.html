How it works

Set up in under a minute.

  1. 1

    Allow notification access

    Android asks you to turn it on once. The onboarding opens the right screen for you.

  2. 2

    Pick your apps

    Choose which apps can ring, then add a sound or filters if you want them.

  3. 3

    Carry on with your day

    When a chosen app notifies you, the alarm plays until you stop it.

Private by design

Your notifications never leave your phone.

No server, no account, no analytics. The only network use is the update check you start yourself. Message text is read only to apply your filters and is never stored or logged. It's open source under the MIT license, so you can check every line.
